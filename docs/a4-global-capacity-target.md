## From actual monomials to admissible rows

Write $s\in\mathbb Z/d$, $T$ for the orbit's top letters, $p$ for the residue prime, and $h_1,h_2,|\nu|$ for first row length, second row length and size of each diagram. Here $d$ is even and at least two. The abstract assembly only requires an integer base at least three. Its arithmetic caller has additional prime and frame hypotheses. Set $D_s=|\nu_s|-1_{s\in T}h_1(s)$ and $c_s=h_2(s)$ on $T$, $h_1(s)$ elsewhere. [Definitions](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/a172c464fd4bcfc4f53e394dcba45d742b1409a6/src/Formalism/IncomingBounds/IncomingBound.lean#L103) · [Exact assembly proposition](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/a172c464fd4bcfc4f53e394dcba45d742b1409a6/src/Formalism/GaussJetBounds/StatementA.lean#L19)

| Index and multiplicity | $I=(\mathbb Z/d)\times\mathrm{Fin}(N_0)\times\mathrm{Fin}(N_1)$, cardinality $dN_0N_1$. A row is $(s,k)$, of which there are $dN_0$. $b$ is an output coordinate. For **each supported monomial** of the product of the diagram column minors, $\kappa_{skb}$ is its exponent. Top means $s\in T,k=0$. Zero exponents stay in the sum. |
| `spec` and `hspec` | For destination $s$, enumerate the odd integers $j$ with $s_0=s-j\in T$. For $j>0$ require $p^j\le h_1(s_0)$, for $j<0$ require $p^{|j|}\le |\nu_s|$. The cap on diagram size $a$ bounds $|j|$ and the count by $2\log_p a+1$. The rank hypothesis $2\log_p a+2\le N_0-1$ leaves room for the reserved row. The injection $e_s$ assigns the enumerated labels to distinct rows, skipping row zero at a top. A generic matrix minor is a unit for these labels. The resulting frame sets `spec(s,k)` to the assigned $(s_0,j)$ on the image, and to none elsewhere. This map is reused for every $b$. |
| `htopsum` and `hnonsum` | A minor of height $h$ has degree $h$. Cauchy–Binet restricts its support to choices of $h$ **distinct** rows. At a top, the fixed first row forces row zero into every positive-height column. Summing over columns gives exactly $h_1(s)$ on that row and $D_s$ on the other rows. Elsewhere the whole degree is $D_s$. A zero minor contributes no supported term. |
| `hcap` | One signed pair determines its destination and at most one assigned row there. Its entire usage, summed over **all** $b$, is bounded by the number of eligible columns. An ordinary row can occur in at most $h_1(s)$ columns. At a top a nonreserved row needs a column of height at least two, giving $h_2(s)$. Thus each fibre has mass at most $c_s$. Distinct signed pairs can have the same destination. They cannot claim the same row, and their combined mass is still bounded by the total degree there. |
| `IsOptDual` | For odd $i<d$ the flow constraints are $\sum_i[p^if_{s,i}+f_{s-i,i}]\le D_s$, $f\ge0$. The objective is $\sum_{s,i}f_{s,i}$. A dual $0\le\sigma\le1$ satisfies $p^i\sigma_s+\sigma_{s+i}\ge1$. The zero flow is feasible and the finite primal optimum is bounded. Finite LP duality supplies an optimizer. A dual coordinate above one can be clipped to one since every positive constraint coefficient is at least one. Hence $F(D)=\gamma\sum_s D_s-\max\sum f=\sum_sD_s(\gamma-\sigma_s)$ for this very $D$. The one-monomial caller obtains it from `lemmaFa`. |

## Indispensable quantitative target

Put $M=k-1$ in the local rank theorem, $N=|TT|$, $Y=x^{13}$, $Y_b=x^\varepsilon/20$. Here $M$ is **not** the number of lifts $m$. For each eligible prime $p$, orbit length $n'$, good pattern $TT\subseteq\mathrm{Fin}(n')$, legal constants and size data, the required large-prime specialization is

$$
\kappa_I(a,b)\ge0,\qquad
\frac{1}{|B_a||B_b|}\sum_{a\in B_a,b\in B_b}\kappa_I(a,b)
\le C_m N N_1/Y .
$$

The local boxes are zero off $TT$. On it they independently range over all integers in $[a_0,a_0+a_0/(kY)]$ and $[b',b'+N_*x^\varepsilon L_a b'/k]$. The constant precedes $x,i,p,n',TT$ in the family theorem. The prime is prime, $p_0(k)\le p<X'$, outside the exceptional band, with a good orbit, $L_2<\mathrm{goodU}(\delta_1)$, $3G_p\le n'$, and the entire predicate `HypI`. This last predicate ties the prime, widths, spacing and precision to the same rank. For example $k^2Y^2\le p<a_0/b'$, $2N_1+2\le k-1$, and $2Y_bNL_a<k$. The full inequalities, including the term in $Q_p^4$ and the odd-power exclusions in the unspecialized theorem, are pinned below. They rule out the tempting $p=5$ global test for large $x$. [Eligible inputs and inequality](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/a172c464fd4bcfc4f53e394dcba45d742b1409a6/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/SmallOrderPrimes.lean#L149) · [Integer boxes](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/a172c464fd4bcfc4f53e394dcba45d742b1409a6/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/IsResidueClassZero.lean#L305) · [Original hypotheses](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/a172c464fd4bcfc4f53e394dcba45d742b1409a6/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/ProjectionLemmaBaseLevel.lean#L40)

The original theorem constructs **one nonnegative defect function on pairs** with mean $\le C N N_1/Y$. It must work for every unramified quadratic setting, allowed dominant base weight, integer shift $t\ge t_I$ and target lattice. The explicit $t_I$ contains the preceding type-V contributions, $p^{-2},p^{-1},k^{-1}$ losses and terms in $p^{G'}$, $c_\star$ and $n'$. The canonical defect in the application is the infimum over defects with this universal property, at $t=\lceil t_{If}\rceil_+$, precision $D_{If}$ and $c_\star=8(D_{If}+1)$. The comparison for the **mean** only needs this infimum to be bounded by the constructed defect. Using the infimum as an admissible rank parameter is a distinct attainment question. [Exact shift and theorem](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/a172c464fd4bcfc4f53e394dcba45d742b1409a6/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/ProjectionLemmaBaseLevel.lean#L66) · [Canonical infimum](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/a172c464fd4bcfc4f53e394dcba45d742b1409a6/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/DefsInst.lean#L38)

For every such lattice $L$, there must be edges targeting $L$ and vectors on their source lattices, isolated to order $c_\star$ on competing edges. On the target module there must be an integral linear map $u$ and matrix $A$ with $A(\langle F_i,u(z)\rangle)_i=p^D z$ for all $z\in O^r$, with $r\ge(1-\kappa)\dim V$. This is an integral interpolation condition even when $p^D$ vanishes in the residue field. Computing the rational rank of one minor cannot determine that defect.
