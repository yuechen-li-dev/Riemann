## Exact quantitative target

Write π=‖p‖, v(c)=log(‖c‖)/log(π) with v(0)=+∞, ρ=1/(p²−1), γ=p/(p²−1), h=1/(p−1), and |n|=Σ nᵢ for total degree. A coefficient has weight v(cₙ)+ρ|n|. Every lower valuation bound below is inclusive. No ceiling is appropriate for an arbitrary element of a ramified extension. The endpoints use the valuation normalization specified there.

* For **every** characteristic-zero ultrametric normed field K, odd prime p with π<1, even d≥2, subfield K₀⊂K with v(K₀ˣ)⊆ℤ, and primitive (pᵈ−1)-th root ζ in K, take any series b on ℤ/d satisfying BHyp. Its coefficients lie in K₀. It is invariant under Xₛ↦ζ^((-1)^s p^s) Xₛ (canonical residues). Every supported monomial has positive degree on each parity and bidegree different from (1,1). Separately split the even or the odd variables into two copies, taking Δb=b(X+X′,Y)−b(X,Y)−b(X′,Y), or its odd counterpart. For each split, exp(Δb) after the letter-log substitution has integral coefficients and constant 1. Letter log sends variable (copy,s) to Σₑ X_(copy,s−2e)^(p^(2e))/pᵉ.
* **LemmaS conclusion.** Every coefficient has weight ≥h−1. It has weight ≥h unless its index equals p^α eₛ+p^β eₜ with s even, t odd, α or β positive, and s+α=t+β mod d. The weaker bound is allowed on this set. The statement asserts no failure of the stronger bound there. [Definitions of weight, split and exceptional index](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/a172c464fd4bcfc4f53e394dcba45d742b1409a6/src/Formalism/GaussJetBounds/Statement.lean#L17) · [BHyp, Unram and full LemmaS](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/a172c464fd4bcfc4f53e394dcba45d742b1409a6/src/Formalism/GaussJetBounds/Statement.lean#L85)
* **LemmaRA additional data.** For every pair of natural ranks N₀,N₁, let z=(s,a,b′) index their d blocks of entries. For any finite index type I with decidable equality and labels λ:I→ℤ/d, use a matrix M with ‖M_zj‖≤1, M_zj≠0 only when λ(j)=s. Put H_z=Σⱼ M_zj Tⱼ, B=Σ_{a,b′} b((H_(s,a,b′))ₛ). Take zero-constant series g_z such that substituting g into each entry log gives its coordinate Z_z. Take any coefficientwise integral U on the entries, sign η=±1, and σ:I→ℝ≥0. Fix κ∈ℕ^I, integer A≥1 with κᵢ≤A. For **every** i,j,α,β with μ=p^α eᵢ+p^β eⱼ≤κ componentwise and v(B_μ)+ρ|μ|<h, require p^α σᵢ+p^β σⱼ≥1. This condition concerns the actual coefficient after the linear substitution. With V=U(g)(H), the conclusion is

  $\displaystyle v\!\left(\left(\prod_i\kappa_i!\right)[T^\kappa]\,V\exp(\eta B)\right)\ge\sum_i\kappa_i(\gamma-\sigma_i)-|I|(\log_p A+1).$

  The criterion for a bad coefficient is strict. The resulting bound and the radius payment ≥1 both admit equality. The estimate is over arbitrary fields/ranks as above, with explicit dimension and size loss. ## What the determinant bound measures

For a prime p≥5, even d≥2, N₀<N₁ and one diagram νₛ of height ≤N₀ per letter, choose a subset T of top letters, a jet series Θ, and for each diagram column an increasing selection of output columns. Form G(X,Z) by multiplying the corresponding minors of XₛZₛ, using the first rows of Xₛ. KvalGe(B) requires, for **every** selection and every X-monomial m,

v(Σᵤ [XᵐZᵘ]G · (∏ᵢ uᵢ!) · [Zᵘ]Θ) ≥ B.

This is a family of coefficient bounds on a differentiated product of minors. Each inner coefficient is a sum; cancellation is permitted. It gives no divisibility statement for each term of that sum or each raw coefficient of Θ. [Jet, ranks and prime](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/a172c464fd4bcfc4f53e394dcba45d742b1409a6/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataJet.lean#L13) · [Minor operator and universal quantifiers](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/a172c464fd4bcfc4f53e394dcba45d742b1409a6/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/Divisibility/ProductZerothFirstParts.lean#L82)

AdmB requires a unit ep, a sign, b obeying B1–B4, and an integral series U such that Θ(ep·jetLog)=U·exp(sign·Σ_entries b)(jetLog). B1 puts coefficients in the closed unramified subalgebra generated by prime-to-p roots of unity. B2 is the torus symmetry, B3 the bidegree exclusion, B4 the two split-exponential integrality identities (over its integral part). The source supplies BHyp through `tieB` and the inverse-log expression through `kernel_from_twist_holds`. The real Hida parameter a must satisfy p≤a, |νₛ|≤a and 2logₚ a+2≤N₀−1. These restrictions on the instance matter even for tiny diagrams. Let Aₛ and Bₛ be the first and second row lengths, Dₛ=|νₛ|−1_{s∈T}Aₛ, cₛ=Bₛ on T and Aₛ elsewhere, and Jₛ the first odd e≥1 with s+e∈T (∞ if absent). Wₛ is the supremum of c_(s−e) over odd e with pᵉ≤|ν_(s−e)|, zero for the empty set. Write

* tₛ=Σ_{odd e<Jₛ} max(Aₛ/pᵉ−c_(s+e),0) + Σ_{odd e>Jₛ} Aₛ/pᵉ.
* bₛ=Σ_{odd e≠Jₛ} max((Aₛ−Bₛ)/pᵉ−c_(s+e),0).
* F(D)=γΣD−max Σ xₛᵢ, where x≥0, i ranges over positive odd integers <d, and $\sum_i(p^i x_{s,i}+x_{s-i,i})\le D_s$ in each cyclic position.
* H=d N₀ N₁(logₚ a+1). ThmA(1) asserts KvalGe(Σ_T(tₛ−γWₛ)+F(D)−H).
* Cor2(11,1) further requires integer k≥3, wₛ>0, 0≤Eₛ≤4wₛ, Dₛ=(k−1)wₛ+Eₛ−2·1_{s∈T}wₛ. Its conclusion is KvalGe(Σ_T bₛ+F((k−1)w)−11Σw/p−γΣ_T Wₛ−H). The separate one-letter theorem is a second component of OrbitLetterBoundsAll. The second-orbit route uses the multi-letter Cor2 projection.
